3 out of 5 stars. This is a frustrating review to write because so much about this hotel was genuinely good, which makes the one major problem we had even more disappointing.

Let's start with the positives, because there were real ones. The room itself was spotless when we checked in, fresh linens, no dust, bathroom clearly deep-cleaned, none of the small grime details that make you side-eye a hotel room. The location could not have been better for what we needed, an eight-minute walk to the main square, close to several good restaurants, and quiet enough at night that street noise was never an issue. The check-in staff were friendly and efficient, and the bed itself was comfortable. If this review ended there, I'd be writing four or five stars without hesitation.

Unfortunately, on the second night of our stay, which happened to be the single hottest night of our entire trip, the air conditioning unit in our room stopped working sometime in the early evening. We noticed the room getting warmer around 9 p.m., called the front desk, and were told someone would come look at it. No one came. We called again around 11 p.m. and were told the same thing. By midnight, the room was uncomfortably hot, genuinely difficult to sleep in, and we called a third time, increasingly frustrated, asking for either a fix or a room change. We were told the hotel was fully booked and there was nothing available to move us to, and that maintenance would come "as soon as possible."

Maintenance actually showed up at around 8 a.m. the next morning, roughly nine hours after our first call. By that point, we had barely slept, had opened the window as a poor substitute for working air conditioning, and had spent the night sweating through a trip we'd been looking forward to for months. The technician fixed the unit within about fifteen minutes, which was almost more frustrating to watch, since it made clear the fix wasn't complicated and could have happened hours earlier had anyone actually prioritized it.

To the hotel's credit, when we mentioned the issue at checkout, the manager on duty apologized and adjusted our bill to remove the charge for that specific night, which I appreciated and which is the main reason this isn't a lower-star review. But a partial refund doesn't really make up for a sleepless night in genuinely uncomfortable heat, especially when three separate phone calls over several hours failed to get anyone to actually come look at the problem until the following morning.

If you're booking here in a season where you'll actually need the air conditioning to work, I'd think carefully, or at least go in with the awareness that if something breaks at night, you may be on your own for longer than you'd expect. The room, the location, and the staff's attitude at checkout were all genuinely good. The middle-of-the-night response time was not, and for a hotel at this price point, it should have been.
