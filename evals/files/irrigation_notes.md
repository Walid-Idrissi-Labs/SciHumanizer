Trial notes: drip irrigation scheduling, Syrah block, 2023 season

- Two plots of 0.8 ha each, same rootstock (110R), planted in 2011
- Plot A: fixed schedule, 3 irrigations per week, 4 h each (the grower's usual practice)
- Plot B: irrigation triggered when soil water tension at 60 cm exceeded 80 kPa (6 tensiometers per plot, read daily at 7:00)
- Season: 1 June to 15 September, 107 days; rainfall 38 mm (30-year mean for the period: 96 mm)
- Water applied: A 1,840 m3/ha; B 1,210 m3/ha
- Yield: A 7.9 t/ha; B 7.6 t/ha (difference not significant, p = 0.41; 12 sampled rows per plot)
- Berry sugar at harvest: A 23.1 °Brix; B 24.0 °Brix
- Pump energy: A 610 kWh/ha; B 405 kWh/ha
- Two tensiometers in plot B failed in July (air entry) and were replaced within 5 days
- Labour for the daily readings: about 20 minutes per day
- One season, one site, sandy-loam soil
