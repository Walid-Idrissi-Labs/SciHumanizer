We compared two ways of scheduling drip irrigation in a Syrah block during the 2023 season. One plot was irrigated on the grower's fixed schedule. The other was irrigated only when the soil water tension at 60 cm exceeded 80 kPa. The tension-based plot received 1,210 m3/ha of water, against 1,840 m3/ha for the fixed schedule. Yield was similar in the two plots (7.6 and 7.9 t/ha), and the difference was not significant. In this trial, scheduling by soil water tension saved water without a measurable loss of yield.
